9: 
10: **The optimistic concurrency check is aimed at the wrong target.** If Antigravity hashes the *live working tree*, you get spurious rejections whenever main moves or a developer touches a file — and on a busy repo that becomes a livelock: Claude re-reads, resubmits, main moves again, rejected again.
